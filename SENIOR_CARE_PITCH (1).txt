───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
📱 WHAT IS SENIOR CARE COMPANION?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Senior Care Companion is an all-in-one health management app designed
specifically for seniors. It helps you manage:

💊 MEDICATIONS
   • Track unlimited medications with custom schedules
   • Multi-dose support (mark each dose individually)
   • Automatic daily reset (no manual unchecking!)
   • Voice commands: "Mark my morning Aspirin as taken"

📅 APPOINTMENTS
   • Doctor visits, lab tests, therapy sessions
   • Reminders before each appointment
   • Notes field for prep instructions
   • Voice: "What appointments do I have this week?"

✅ DAILY TASKS
   • Meals, hydration, hygiene, safety checks
   • Organized by category, auto-refreshing
   • Voice: "Did I eat breakfast?"

💰 BILLS
   • Track due dates and payments
   • Recurring bills automatically renew
   • Never miss a payment again

🎉 ACTIVITIES
   • Social events, classes, gatherings
   • Attendance tracking
   • Stay socially engaged

📞 CONTACTS
   • Important phone numbers in one place
   • One-tap calling
   • Emergency 911 button on home screen

🎤 VOICE ASSISTANT
   • 50+ voice commands
   • Hands-free operation
   • Great for limited mobility or vision

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🎯 WHY WAS THIS CREATED?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE PROBLEM:
Many seniors are managing:
• Multiple medications (some 2-3 times daily)
• Regular doctor appointments and lab visits
• Daily routines like meals, hydration, safety checks
• Bills and payments to track
• Social activities to stay engaged

They're doing this across:
• Multiple apps (confusing!)
• Paper calendars and sticky notes (easy to lose!)
• Memory alone (hard to track everything!)

Existing apps have problems:
❌ Too complicated for non-tech-savvy users
❌ Expensive monthly subscriptions
❌ Require constant internet connection
❌ Collect and sell personal health data
❌ Not designed for seniors' actual needs

OUR SOLUTION:
An app that's:
✅ Simple - Designed for seniors, not tech experts
✅ Voice-enabled - 50+ commands for hands-free use
✅ Private - All data stays on your phone, never sent anywhere
✅ Offline-first - Works without internet after installation
✅ iPhone optimized - Add to home screen, works like a native app

📲 HOW TO INSTALL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IPHONE INSTALLATION (EASIEST):
1. Save the attached file to your iPhone
2. Open it in Safari (the blue compass icon)
3. Tap the Share button ⬆️ at the bottom
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" in the top right
6. Done! App icon appears on your home screen

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Must use Safari (not Chrome)

DETAILED HELP:
The app has a complete installation guide in:
Help → 📱 Add to Home Screen

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Yes! All data stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
