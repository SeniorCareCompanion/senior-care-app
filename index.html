🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to Senior Care! 👋

This app helps you manage medications, appointments, tasks, bills, activities, and contacts all in one place.

🏠 Understanding the Home Screen:

🔔 Today's Reminders

The Home screen shows a "Today's Reminders" card listing everything you need to do today, sorted by time:

  • 💊 Medications to take
  • 📅 Appointments scheduled
  • ✅ Tasks to complete
  • 💰 Bills due soon (within 7 days)

💡 Quick View: Shows first 5 reminders

If you have more than 5 reminders, you'll see a purple line that says "...and X more" with a down arrow (▼). Click this line to expand and see ALL your reminders for the day! Click the up arrow (▲) to collapse back to the top 5.

📱 Your First Steps:

  1. Add to Home Screen: Tap here for Installation Guide (most important first step!)
  2. Home Screen: See today's overview - medications to take, upcoming appointments, tasks to complete
  3. Navigation Tabs: Tap the tabs at the top to switch between sections (Meds, Appts, Tasks, etc.)
  4. Add Your First Item: Go to any section and fill in the form at the bottom
  5. Set Reminders: Check the "Enable Reminder" box to get notifications
  6. Use Expand/Collapse: Each page has "Expand All" and "Collapse All" buttons at the top

🚨 SAFETY FIRST - Emergency 911:

⚠️ IMPORTANT: There is a large red "🚨 CALL 911 EMERGENCY" button on the Home screen.

How to Call 911:

  1. Go to Home tab (🏠)
  2. Tap the red "🚨 CALL 911 EMERGENCY" button at the top
  3. Confirm when asked

Use in Real Emergencies Only:

  • Medical emergencies (heart attack, stroke, severe bleeding)

  • ⚠️ Confirmation prevents accidental calls

💡 For more details, see Help → Contacts → Emergency Call section

🔧 Troubleshooting:

Don't see "Add to Home Screen" option?

  1. Make sure you're in Safari (not Chrome, Firefox, or another browser)
  2. Update Safari: Make sure your iPhone is running the latest iOS version
  3. Scroll in the Share menu: The option might be hidden below - scroll down!
  4. Enable JavaScript: Go to iPhone Settings → Safari → Advanced → Enable JavaScript

App icon disappeared from home screen?

  1. Search for it: Swipe down on home screen and type "Senior Care"
  2. Check App Library: Swipe left past your last home screen page
  3. Re-add it: If lost, just follow the installation steps again

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Will I lose my data if I delete the icon?

A: No! Your data is stored in Safari's storage, not in the icon. Deleting and re-adding the icon won't affect your data.

Q: Can I use this on Android?

A: Yes! In Chrome on Android, tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"

Q: Do I need an app store account?

A: No! This is a web app, so no App Store needed. Just add directly to your home screen.

Q: Will it use my phone's storage?

A: Very minimal storage (just a few MB for the icon and your data).

🎯 Understanding Confirmations:

The app recognizes 25+ ways to say YES and NO!

✅ YES variations:

yes, yeah, yep, yup, sure, ok, okay, confirm, correct, right, affirmative, go, do it, proceed, uh-huh, absolutely, definitely, indeed, please, continue, accept

❌ NO variations:

no, nope, nah, cancel, stop, don't, never, negative, nevermind, decline, reject, no way, not now, abort

🎯 How Voice Commands Work:

Smart Parsing: The app tries to understand everything you say in one command.

Guided Conversation: If you're missing information, the app will ask questions.

Always Confirm: Before taking any action, the app will ask "Is this correct?"

Example Conversation:

You: "Add medication"

App: "What medication would you like to add?"

You: "Aspirin"

App: "Aspirin. What dosage?"

You: "81 milligrams"

App: "81mg. What time do you take it?"

You: "8 AM"

App: "I will add Aspirin 81mg at 8:00 AM daily. Is this correct?"

You: "Yes"

App: "Added Aspirin 81mg at 8:00 AM"

💡 Tips for Best Results:

  • Be specific: "Add medication Aspirin 81mg at 8 AM" is better than just "add medication"
  • Speak clearly: Reduce background noise
  • Wait for red: Button turns red when listening
  • Say yes or no: When asked for confirmation
  • Be patient: App may ask follow-up questions

⚠️ Important Notes:

  • Voice assistant always confirms before taking actions
  • You can say "cancel" or "no" at any time
  • Works best in quiet environments
  • Uses fuzzy and phonetic matching to understand variations

📱 Important: iOS/iPhone Limitation

The app must be open and visible to show reminders. This is an iOS limitation for web apps.

What this means:

  • ✅ App open on screen = reminders work
  • ❌ App in background = reminders paused
  • ❌ Phone locked/sleeping = reminders paused
  • ✅ Opening app again = reminders resume

💡 Tip: Keep the app open on your screen during times when you need reminders, or check the app regularly to see what's due.

ℹ️ About This App

Why Senior Care Companion Exists

Senior Care Companion was created to solve a simple but important problem: helping seniors manage their health and daily life without the complexity, cost, or privacy concerns of traditional apps.

The Problem We're Solving:

Many seniors are managing:

  • Multiple medications with different schedules (some 2-3 times daily)
  • Regular doctor appointments, lab visits, and therapy sessions
  • Daily tasks like meals, hydration, and safety checks
  • Bills and payments to track
  • Social activities to stay engaged

Existing solutions are either too complicated, require expensive subscriptions, need constant internet, or collect and sell personal health data.

Our Solution:

An all-in-one app that's:

  • ✅ Simple - Designed specifically for seniors, not tech experts
  • 🎤 Voice-enabled - 50+ commands for hands-free use
  • 🔒 Private - All data stays on your phone, never sent anywhere
  • 📴 Offline-first - Works without internet after installation
  • 📱 iPhone optimized - Add to home screen, works like a native app

Key Features:

💊 Medications: Track unlimited meds, multi-dose support, automatic daily reset

📅 Appointments: Never miss a doctor visit with reminders

✅ Tasks: Daily routines organized by category (meals, hydration, hygiene, safety)

💰 Bills: Track payments and recurring bills

🎉 Activities: Stay socially engaged with event tracking

📞 Contacts: Important numbers with one-tap calling

🚨 Emergency: Prominent 911 button on home screen

Built With Care:

Every feature was designed with seniors in mind. Large text, simple navigation, voice commands for accessibility, comprehensive help documentation, and no frustrating re-logins. This app stays logged in even when your phone sleeps, so it's always ready when you need it.
