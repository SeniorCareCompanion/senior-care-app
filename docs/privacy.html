PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated May 30, 2026

Senior Care Companion takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Key Points:

  • We collect names, emails, usernames, passwords, and medication data
  • We use this information to provide and improve our Services
  • We send notifications to family members to help seniors stay on track with medications
  • We do NOT sell or share your data with third parties
  • We comply with all applicable privacy laws

Information We Collect

When you register and use Senior Care Companion, we collect:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials (username, password)
  • Medication information and adherence data
  • Family connection information
  • Device and usage information

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide medication reminders and tracking
  • Send notifications to authorized family members
  • Improve and optimize our Services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

Your Rights:

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request a copy of your data

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as long as necessary to provide our Services. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, and only under strict confidentiality agreements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
