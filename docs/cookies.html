COOKIE POLICY

Last updated May 30, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your device (computer, mobile phone, or tablet) when you visit our website or use our application. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the Service to function properly, such as authentication and security. Without these, certain features may not work.
  • Preference Cookies: These remember your choices and settings to provide a personalized experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how users interact with our Service so we can improve it and fix issues.
  • Marketing Cookies: These may be used to track your activity and show you relevant advertising or content.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Maintain your login session and user authentication
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of our Service
  • Provide security features and detect fraud or unauthorized access
  • Personalize content and recommendations

Third-Party Cookies

Some content or services on our website may be provided by third parties. These third parties may place their own cookies on your device. We are not responsible for the cookies placed by third parties and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

How to Control Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all cookies
  • Be notified when a cookie is being set
  • Delete cookies after you close your browser

Important: Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our Service. Some features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Cookie Duration

Cookies may be either "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what cookies are being used
  • Control which cookies are placed on your device
  • Request information about our use of cookies
  • Request deletion of cookies
